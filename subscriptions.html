Subscriptions, Cancellations & Refunds

Updated: November 16, 2026

SetRelay offers free features and optional SetRelay Pro monthly and yearly subscriptions purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase system. This page describes subscription management and the support process.

Pricing and renewal

Current prices, billing periods and offers are displayed in the App Store purchase confirmation before you subscribe. SetRelay Pro subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. Apple processes purchases and renewals. Actual pricing and taxes may vary based on your App Store region.

Cancel or manage a subscription

On an iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SetRelay to manage or cancel your plan. You can also use Apple's subscription management page. Cancel before your next renewal to avoid a new charge, in accordance with Apple's subscription terms. Canceling ordinarily leaves access available until the current paid period ends. Deleting the app or your SetRelay account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

Restore Purchases

If SetRelay Pro is not showing after a valid purchase, sign in to the SetRelay account used for the subscription and choose Restore Purchases from the app's subscription settings. SetRelay account access and Apple purchase restoration are distinct: a purchase is not automatically transferable to a different SetRelay account. If you cannot access your original SetRelay account, contact support for account recovery assistance. Do not share passwords or payment-card information.

Refunds

For App Store purchases, request a refund using Apple's Report a Problem service. Apple handles App Store refund requests under its applicable policies. You can also contact SetRelay support for assistance. Nothing on this page limits rights provided by applicable consumer-protection law.

Questions and complaints

Contact support@setrelay.app with the issue, the affected feature, and the date of the transaction if relevant. We will review the report and respond by email. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or full payment details. See our support page for other issues.
