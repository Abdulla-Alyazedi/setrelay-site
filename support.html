SetRelay Support

Support for the SetRelay iPhone app

If you need help with your account, workouts, subscriptions, data, Yo-Yo testing, or another SetRelay feature, contact us with enough detail to reproduce the issue.

Contact support

support@setrelay.app

Please do not include passwords, authentication codes, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive credentials.

Helpful information to include

  • SetRelay app version
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • The screen or feature where the issue occurred
  • Steps that caused the issue
  • A screenshot or screen recording if it contains no sensitive information

Subscriptions, refunds and complaints

Manage or cancel Apple subscriptions through your iPhone Settings or Apple subscriptions. For purchase restoration, refund requests and account recovery, read our Subscriptions, Cancellations & Refunds page.

To submit a complaint, email support@setrelay.app with a description of the issue. We will review it and respond by email. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.
